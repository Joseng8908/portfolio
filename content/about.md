**Why this site exists**

Most of what I learn gets lost between a terminal scrollback buffer and a
half-finished note. This is the durable version: things I measured, things that
surprised me, and the reasoning I would otherwise have to reconstruct from
scratch six months later.

Posts are tagged by subsystem — `RDMA`, `NUMA`, `scheduling`, `kubernetes`,
`go`, and so on. The [tag index]({{< relref "/tags" >}}) is the fastest way in.
